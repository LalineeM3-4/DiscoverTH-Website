Food & Drink

Thailand offers a world-famous culinary experience that balances spicy, sweet, sour, and salty flavors.
  • Pad Kra Prow : A savory stir-fry with holy basil and minced meat, usually served over rice with a fried egg.
  • Mango Sticky Rice : Sweet sticky rice topped with fresh mango slices and rich coconut milk.
  • Nom Yen: Milky iced drink flavored with sweet sala syrup.
  • Thai Beers: Popular lagers like Singha, Chang, and Leo that pair well with spicy food.
🚆

Getting Around

Thailand offers many transport options including trains, buses, taxis, boats and local tuk-tuks. Bangkok also has convenient BTS and MRT systems.
🛕

Culture & Customs

The Wai is Thailand’s traditional greeting, performed by pressing your palms together in a prayer-like position and slightly bowing your head. When visiting Thai temples, it is essential to adhere to certain customs and manners to show respect for the sacred spaces.
  • Cover your shoulders and knees.
  • Remove shoes before entering when required.
  • Dress modestly at temples.
  • Show respect during the national anthem.
💡

Travel Tips

  • Carry some Thai baht for smaller purchases.
  • Stay hydrated, especially during hot weather.
  • Respect local customs and traditions.
  • Keep important travel documents secure.
📍

Popular Areas

Thailand's most popular areas include bustling cities, cultural hubs, and tropical island paradises.
  • Bangkok : The vibrant capital famous for grand temples like Wat Arun and massive shopping malls like Siam Paragon.
  • Chiang Mai : The cultural heart of the north, known for mountain views, night markets, and ancient temples.
  • Phuket : A major island destination with lively beaches, resorts, and Sino-Portuguese old town architecture.
  • Krabi : A scenic coastal province featuring dramatic limestone cliffs and clear waters at Railay Beach.
  • Ko Samui : A tropical island in the Gulf of Thailand offering luxury resorts, palm-fringed beaches, and quiet coves.
